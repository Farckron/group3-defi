How it works

  1. Add liquidity on Uniswap v2 (your token + GROUP3). You receive LP tokens.
  2. Approve the farm to use your LP tokens, then Stake them here.
  3. While the campaign is active, each pool pays a fixed amount of GROUP3 per second, split between stakers by their share of the pool.
  4. Claim collects earned GROUP3. Withdraw returns your LP tokens (also after the campaign ends).
  5. To get your original tokens back, remove liquidity separately: Uniswap positions, or call removeLiquidity on the Router02 on Etherscan (approve the Router for your LP first).
Testnet only (Sepolia). These tokens have no monetary value and rewards are not real profit. This page never asks for a private key or seed phrase.
